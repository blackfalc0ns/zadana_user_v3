## 6. Prices, delivery fees, tax, and cash-on-delivery fees

Before confirmation, you will see an order summary that normally includes: the product value, any discount, delivery fees, value-added tax where enabled for the area, and cash-on-delivery fees where applicable.

Delivery fees are calculated operationally according to the delivery route and the active pricing policy, including distance, area, peak periods, and other factors. The total delivery fee may be shown without full internal details.

Value-added tax and cash-on-delivery fees are determined by the delivery-address area and Platform settings at the time of the order.

Cash-on-delivery fees apply only when cash on delivery is selected, if that fee is enabled.

Changing the payment method or address may change the total. You must rely on the latest summary displayed by the server before confirmation.

Discount codes apply in accordance with the offer terms stated in the app.

The estimated time of arrival (ETA) is an estimate within a time window and is not a binding delivery-time promise.

## 8. Order fulfillment, tracking, and delivery

After payment or order confirmation, according to the selected method, an order may proceed through stages including merchant acceptance, preparation, courier assignment, collection from the merchant, in transit, and delivery.

Merchant acceptance is an essential part of fulfillment. The merchant may reject the order or part of it according to stock and availability.

During tracking, the app may display information about the assigned courier, such as name, contact method, and vehicle details, as well as the courier's location, only while there is an active assignment related to your order.

A delivery code may be shown when available. You must share it with the courier at handover to complete delivery.

Your visible reference is the order number displayed in the app.

## 10. Complaints, refund requests, and support

You may open a support case for a complaint or refund request approximately under the following conditions:

There is a maximum number of open support cases on your account, currently up to five cases.

A case may go through stages such as: submitted, under review, awaiting documents from you, approved, rejected, or resolved.

The result of a refund or compensation request is determined after review. Compensation may be a monetary amount, a coupon, or another settlement method under the Platform policy.
